Personal playlist player

Northstar plays your compatible HTTPS playlist. No channels, premium subscriptions or resale licenses are sold here. The sample collection is for testing playback.

Where the collection comes from

Live sample links are drawn from the public IPTV-org directory. Their availability and redistribution rights are not verified. Open-film credits are shown in the player. The official viewing guide opens broadcaster websites separately. Their own accounts, subscriptions and regional restrictions apply.

Connect another playlist

Use a direct HTTPS M3U link or a playlist file. Provider login portals and Xtream APIs need a future backend integration. Favorites stay in this browser; imported stream links are not saved after a reload.

TV controls: use arrow keys to move between controls, Enter to select and Escape/Back to close a player. Browser and device support varies.
